Final investigation

You have completed seven connected Zombie Files investigations about the Ravenwood outbreak. The Research Division now needs a final scientific verdict on Subject Z-02. Use the evidence and biology you have already studied. This assessment does not introduce a new subject.

This is a 50-point summative assessment with five parts worth 10 points each. There is no time limit. You can go back and revise earlier parts at any time before you create your Final Case File.

The five parts

Most questions are choice, matching, or evidence-interpretation items. You will write one short explanation (2 to 3 sentences) in each of Parts 1 to 4. Part 5 is the main writing task: a Claim, Evidence, and Reasoning response.

  1. Review the Ravenwood Evidence (10 points)
  2. Investigate the Energy Problem (10 points)
  3. Analyze Body System Failures (10 points)
  4. Evaluate Competing Explanations (10 points)
  5. Deliver the Final Scientific Verdict (10 points)
