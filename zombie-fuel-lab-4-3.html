4.3 Zombie Fuel Lab: Follow the Energy

Ravenwood Biological Investigation | Subject Z-02

How does glucose availability affect a muscle cell's ability to meet ATP demand?

Mission and Variables

Subject Z-02 keeps moving even though its body is badly damaged. Your job is to test a simplified model of a muscle cell and see how fuel availability changes the cell's ability to regenerate ATP. You will compare each result to the cell's ATP demand.

  • Change: glucose availability
  • Measure: ATP regeneration rate
  • Keep constant: oxygen, ATP demand, and trial duration

Simplified educational model. These values are invented simulation units, not real physiological measurements. Real cells can also use other fuels, such as fatty acids. This model deliberately isolates glucose availability so you can study one variable at a time.

Your Prediction

As glucose availability decreases, what do you predict will happen to modeled ATP regeneration?

Predictions are not graded. Compare your prediction to your evidence after you run the trials.

🧪 How to Complete Your Investigation

  1. Predict: Choose what you think will happen as glucose availability decreases.
  2. Experiment: Select High glucose and click Run Trial.
  3. Repeat: Run trials using Medium and Low glucose.
  4. Analyze: Compare the ATP regeneration results in your Trial Log.
  5. Submit: Return to Canvas and answer the two questions in the assignment's Text Entry box.

Analyze Your Evidence

Question 1 (3 points)

What happened to modeled ATP regeneration as glucose availability decreased? Use at least two results from your trials as evidence.

Question 2 (2 points)

Based on your results, explain why Z-02 could not sustain high-intensity movement indefinitely without an adequate supply of usable fuel. Include ATP demand and ATP regeneration in your explanation.

Need a Hint?
  • Question 1: Look at your trial log. Describe the direction of change from High to Low, and quote the ATP regenerated for at least two conditions.
  • Question 2: Compare ATP regenerated to the ATP demand of 60. Think about what the model shows when regeneration is lower than demand, and what that means for a cell that keeps working.

💻 How to Submit Your Lab

You will answer TWO questions worth 5 points total.

  1. Complete all three glucose trials.
  2. Review your Trial Log.
  3. Return to your Canvas assignment.
  4. Click Start Assignment or Submit Assignment.
  5. Type your answers to Questions 1 and 2 in the Text Entry box.
  6. Click Submit Assignment.

Important: Running the simulation does not submit your assignment. You must submit your two answers in Canvas to receive credit.
